== Why

`terminal-gui-prompts` elevates interactive CLI confirm/input to native GUI dialogs when a TTY has a desktop, while always echoing the prompt text to stdout for pipes and automation. That is shell/CLI UX tooling — it belongs with OpenShellOrg (better shells and CLIs), not Dev-Centr (dev-machine orchestration and toolchain equivalence).

== What changed

* Canonical home: https://github.com/openshellorg/terminal-gui-prompts
